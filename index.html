AI × Film

What I actually do with it.

AI video, generative VFX and previs are working tools here, not demos. I shoot live‑action and composite generated elements into it, and I can write the systems behind the workflow as easily as I can use them.

Generation
Google Flow, Veo, Runway, Omni. Text‑to‑video, image‑to‑video, video restyling. Working knowledge of where each model breaks: faces, hands, continuity across cuts.
Hybrid VFX
Compositing generated elements into live‑action plates. Environment work and set extension, and match‑cutting a practical timeline against a generated one.
Consistency
Character and look consistency across a sequence. Prompt architecture, shot‑by‑shot generation order, and canary‑testing the cheapest clip before spending credits on the expensive one.
Post
Premiere Pro, CapCut, Descript. Edit, sound and final delivery.
